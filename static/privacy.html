Privacy

This app collects substantial Discord API data, including messages, user information, and server data.

Per Discord's terms of service and the GDPR, you are entitled to access, update, or delete your personal information.

This button allows you to anonymize all your data in the app. Message content and metadata (such as timestamps) will be kept, but your association to them will be removed.

More precisely, all your messages will be linked to a generic, anonymous user ID, common for all deleted users, and your user account entry will be removed. Keep in mind that this only applies to past data; future data will not be affected until you choose to anonymize it.

If you need further privacy assistance, such as full access or deletion of your data, please send an email to tg-archive-privacy@mail.ewenlau.net. Per GDPR regulation, you will receive a response within one month. If you do not receive a response, please check your spam folder or contact me again.

For more information about what data is collected, and how it is used, please refer to the privacy policy.

The full privacy policy can be found here.
